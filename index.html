About

I'm currently a Staff Engineer in R&D at Synopsys. My doctoral work at IIT Kanpur (PMRF) developed coupled DSMC–DEM and CFD–DEM solvers to study plume–surface interactions and dusty gas dynamics, with applications to lunar landing and particle-laden flows. I have experience in Fortran, C++, Python, OpenFOAM, SPARTA, LIGGGHTS, LAMMPS, and HPC.

Research Interests

  • Dusty gas / gas–granular flows
  • Plume–surface interaction and lunar landing physics
  • CFD–DEM and DSMC–DEM solver development
  • High‑performance computing & parallel solvers
  • Shock–particle interactions, rarefied gas dynamics
